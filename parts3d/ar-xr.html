    What to try (?help)

  1. Enter AR, sweep the phone over the floor until the green ring shows, tap to place.
  2. Bottom row: the round … button (part actions, Undo, Redo), Scene, Add, Option, and the round Mode button (tap it, pick Select, Mark or Measure). The model must not jump when you use any button.
  3. Height up and down: the model moves 0.5 in and stays where you left it.
  4. Nudge arrows: up moves away from you, left and right follow your view. Walk around and try again.
  5. Turn: 5 degrees per tap. Aim the crosshair at a part (beam + tint), press Select or tap the part: the context row shows its controls (lug: length, rotate, gauge; busbar: cover, rotate; nut: none). Tap empty space or Deselect to go back.
  6. While the ring shows, tap the scene to place; tapping the rows must not place.
  7. The Mode button (bottom right) opens a list: Select, Mark, Measure. Mark: Drop (or tap the scene) drops a dot; its label shows when you select it or aim within 2 cm. Measure: tap A then B for a line with the distance. Units and Theme are in the menu (top right, outside AR). Alerts show as toasts at the top.
  8. Preview editor (this page, no AR): click a part to select, drag to orbit, Mark and Measure by clicking the model. Add part (menu) opens the parts dialog (search, preview, Add); stud parts show pulsing rings on compatible studs (click one or use Next, then Place); surface parts drop where you click.
  9. Place again: ring returns, tap a new spot. Reset: offsets and parts back to default.
  10. Exit AR and check the page returns. Compare the battery with the real one for size.
