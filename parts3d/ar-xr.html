    What to try (?help)

  1. Enter AR, sweep the phone over the floor until the green ring shows, tap to place.
  2. Tap the round button (bottom right): the stack of actions must stay on screen after placing and collapses after 4 s, and the model must not jump when you use any button.
  3. Height up and down: the model moves 0.5 in and stays where you left it.
  4. Nudge arrows: up moves away from you, left and right follow your view. Walk around and try again.
  5. Turn: 5 degrees per tap. Aim the crosshair at a part (beam + tint), press Select or tap the part: the round button shows its name and the stack its controls (lug: length, rotate, gauge; busbar: cover, rotate; nut: none). Tap empty space or Deselect to go back.
  6. While the ring shows, tap the scene to place; tapping the round button or stack must not place.
  7. Place again: ring returns, tap a new spot. Reset: offsets and parts back to default.
  8. Exit AR and check the page returns. Compare the battery with the real one for size.
