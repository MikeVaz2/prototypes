    What to try (?help)

  1. Enter AR, sweep the phone over the floor until the green ring shows, tap to place.
  2. Two rows stick to the bottom: Mode, Add, Undo, More; above them the context row (actions or a tooltip line). The model must not jump when you use any button.
  3. Height up and down: the model moves 0.5 in and stays where you left it.
  4. Nudge arrows: up moves away from you, left and right follow your view. Walk around and try again.
  5. Turn: 5 degrees per tap. Aim the crosshair at a part (beam + tint), press Select or tap the part: the context row shows its controls (lug: length, rotate, gauge; busbar: cover, rotate; nut: none). Tap empty space or Deselect to go back.
  6. While the ring shows, tap the scene to place; tapping the rows must not place.
  7. Mode (bottom row) cycles Select, Mark, Measure. Mark: Drop (or tap the scene) drops a dot; its label shows when you select it or aim within 2 cm. Measure: tap A then B for a line with the distance. Units and Theme are in the menu (top right, outside AR). Alerts show as toasts at the top.
  8. Preview editor (this page, no AR): click a part to select, drag to orbit, Mark and Measure by clicking the model. Add part (bottom row) lists the library; stud parts show pulsing rings on compatible studs (click one or use Next, then Place); surface parts drop where you click.
  9. Place again: ring returns, tap a new spot. Reset: offsets and parts back to default.
  10. Exit AR and check the page returns. Compare the battery with the real one for size.
