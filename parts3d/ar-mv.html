Test checklist (on your phone, in AR)
  1. Tap the AR button, place the model on the floor. The strip handle “Adjust” must stay at the bottom before and after placing.
  2. Tap the handle: two rows of big buttons, covering at most a quarter of the screen. Tap again to collapse.
  3. Tap Up½ three times, then nudge ← → ↑ ↓ and turn. The model must move and stay put (no drop back to the floor).
  4. Change extension +1", switch the lug (+/−), rotate it, toggle Cover. The model reloads: it must not jump, sink or lose the offsets.
  5. Tap buttons repeatedly: the model must not be re-placed under your finger.
  6. Drag the model with a finger and twist with two fingers (stock gestures). Then Reset.
  7. Exit AR and re-enter: the page must still work. Note anything that snaps back, with which button.
